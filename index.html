About the TDG-AIL Project

Developing discipline-specific approaches to AI literacy for students at HKU.

Project overview

The TDG-AIL project aims to develop curriculum materials and assessment instruments for a new discipline-specific AI Literacy course, and to evaluate its effectiveness in improving students’ AI literacy. The project is designed for BSc(IM) and BASC(SDS) students at HKU, supporting informed, ethical, and creative engagement with AI in their studies and future work.

People

  • Project Leader: Dr. Jeremy Ng.
  • Collaborator: Prof. Shihui Feng.
  • Student Co-Investigators: Timothy Tang, Clinton Chan, Kenny Chow, and Ngai Poon.
